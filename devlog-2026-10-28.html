2026.10.28DEVELOPMENT UPDATE 03

A colony built to last: the 0.5 update

Trade, new production chains, clearer controls and a faster growing colony.

Beyond our own settlement

Mars now has a regional map, faction relationships and visiting trade caravans. Survivors arrive with goods, while an operating satellite communicator lets the colony trade with an allied orbital station during communication windows. Gold, measured in grams, provides a currency alongside exchanging goods.

Relationships matter in combat too. Allied and neutral visitors have distinct outlines, and defensive systems distinguish them from hostile raiders and AI worshippers.

More ways to supply and defend the base

The latest 0.5 builds add automatic turrets supplied with ammunition, liquefiers for mixing water and solid materials, and electrolyzers for separating liquids into useful outputs. New chemical recipes, salt and better meals give production a closer connection to everyday colony life.

Version 0.5.3 gives liquefiers separate input and output liquid storage, with a pipe outlet for the finished product. Ammunition moves to the consumables section of the item panel, and titanium, magnesium and salt receive clearer categories.

Learning the colony, one step at a time

The tutorial introduces the main interface, drafting, movement, attacks and rescue. Portrait tooltips show vital needs; double-clicking a portrait brings the camera to that colonist. Resource search helps locate explored supplies, while production plans and pipe previews make intent easier to see.

Core play and tutorial text are available in Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Russian and Spanish. We are continuing to improve translation quality and interface layout.

Keeping a larger colony moving

We investigated a real Sol 60 colony and reduced repeated hauling calculations, moved independent gas-chunk work to workers, and removed a costly repeated lookup in pipe rendering. A matching 60-second, 8× simulation test on a Ryzen 7 9800X3D and RTX 5070 Ti improved from about 38 to 98 average FPS at 1080p. This is one measured scenario, not a guarantee for every colony; occasional frames still exceeded the 60 FPS frame budget.

A recent save also exposed a mining issue: an old deceased character record could incorrectly block terrain removal elsewhere. The 0.5.3 fix keeps living-character safety checks while removing that unrelated obstruction. Existing saves are preserved, and the load menu offers a separate 0.5.3 copy for the new liquid-storage rules.

The road ahead

The 0.5 series is our Early Access foundation, with stability, long-term resource flow and combat balance still receiving attention. The game remains in prerelease while Steam build review is underway.

For the next milestone, we are planning neural-chip hacking that unlocks special technology, followed by a small escape rocket and a journey to a stable human settlement. These are future development goals, not features already available in this build.
