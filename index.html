A MARTIAN COLONY SURVIVAL SIM

The surface is only the beginning.

Build a colony beneath the Martian surface. Guide individual survivors, dig into layered geology, grow food, and manage oxygen, heat, water and power as your underground settlement expands.

Ares Program is a single-player 2D simulation currently in development. The footage below was captured from version 0.4.2. Version 0.4.4 adds recreation and further refinements; features and presentation may change.

01

Build below the surface

Excavate layered terrain, move resources and connect rooms into a growing underground settlement. Each new world begins with a procedural map.

02

Keep the systems running

Route gas and liquid through pipes. Balance oxygen, temperature and power. Turn raw materials into the supplies your colony needs.

03

Care for your people

Set schedules and priorities for individual colonists. Grow crops, prepare meals and provide rest while developing skills and researching new facilities.

04

Defend the people you know

Draft colonists, choose combat policies and equip weapons. Face raiders and AGI scouts, rescue the wounded and treat injuries to individual body parts.
